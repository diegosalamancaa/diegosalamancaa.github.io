#

Build Lead @ Penn High Power Rocketry

In 2024–25 I concentrated on fin design and manufacturing for our high-power rocketry team. I helped engineer the fin-can structure, supported aerodynamic performance work with OpenRocket and RASAero, and used Ansys FEA/CFD to evaluate external components like the fin can, boattail, and air-brakes. On the construction side I executed epoxy layups and designed and built my own H-impulse rocket for my L1 certification.

For 2025–26, I’m leading the Fin Can subteam, tying analysis to a repeatable build process. I ran RASAero/OpenRocket trades to set stability margins and ascent loads and completed an in depth flutter analysis to size thickness, sweep, and other parameters by modeling flights on MATLAB to determine critical flutter velocity. To make the design manufacturable at scale, I created a dedicated alignment jig and a documented layup workflow (prep, ply order, cure, and checks).

Additionally, I mentored a set of new members throuhout the design and construction of their own L1 rockets, hosting rocketry, CAD, and other software lessons.
