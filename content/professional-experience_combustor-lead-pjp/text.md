#

Combustor Lead @ Penn Jet Propulsion

###

Mark II - Axial Turbojet

On the Mark II axial micro-turbojet, I worked to design and develop the kerosene fuel-injection system. My work covered injector/manifold layout, basic sizing from combustor requirements, and integration with the liner and ECU start sequence. The Mark II was the first axial turbojet built by an undergraduate team in the United States.

Mark III - Alternative Fuel (Hydrogen) Radial Turbofan

As combustor lead for the Mark III project, an annular combustion chamber for a hydrogen axial micro turbofan engine, I am driving a custom hydrogen combustor from concept to validated design. I defined airflow splits and stabilization concepts, met with various experts regarding dilution hole patterns and used CFD to develop an effective combustion chamber. I run structured design reviews, manage manufacturability and safety constraints, and coordinate with the compressor,and exit nozzle teams to maximize performance. Mark III is still under development.
