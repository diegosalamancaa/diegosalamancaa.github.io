#

Thermoelectrics Intern @ University of Maryland

At UMBC's FlexMeshed Lab with Prof. Deepa Madan, I focused on the design and testing of thermoelectric strips. I worked to refine a small test setup for Seebeck measurements, used MATLAB to process conductivity data gathered from hall effect testing, and iterated on geometry and fabrication steps to improve consistency. I focused on thermoelectric film characterization and maximizing the efficiency of films. I also experimented with MXene-based formulations and inks, comparing mixes and processing conditions to see how they affected electrical and thermal conductivity and overall device performance.

In parallel, I worked on zinc battery projects. This included preparing cells, running basic cycling and impedance checks, and documenting results to guide small design changes.
